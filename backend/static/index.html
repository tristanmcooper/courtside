    What metrics feed these insights?
    Court node · ESP32surface (IR sand) temp, air temp, humidity, wind & audio proxy
    Recovery · Apple Watch / OuraHRV (SDNN / RMSSD), resting HR, sleep duration, respiratory rate
    Workout · Apple Watchin-session avg & max HR, active energy (kcal), duration, prior 7-day load
    Weather · Open-Meteowind speed & gusts, temperature, humidity (independent of the node)
    Player state · self-reportenergy, soreness, mental, warm-up (1–5), food timing
    Context · self-reportpartner, opponent, location, felt wind, felt state, notes
    Outcome modeledself rating (0–10); also peer/coach rating, kills − errors

    The offline pipeline ranks these against the outcome via correlation → Lasso (LOOCV) → random-forest / gradient-boosting → SHAP. At small N, treat rankings as exploratory, not confirmatory.
